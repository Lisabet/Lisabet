# Hi, I'm Lisa Tribunska

I work with entrepreneurs and business leaders on questions about markets, sales, marketing, and user behavior. At my Atelier, I combine research, data analysis, and AI-assisted workflows to understand what's happening and decide what to do next.

Here I share tools and agent skills developed through that work. You can use them in your own projects and adapt them to your workflow.

## Explore the projects

- [Codex Skills](https://github.com/Lisabet/codex-skills) — practical skills for working with an AI assistant, including bringing a project session to a close and preserving what you've learned.
- [Business Economics Kit](https://github.com/Lisabet/business-economics-kit) — two Excel models and an agent skill for exploring the economics of a business idea.
- [Yandex Webvisor Review](https://github.com/Lisabet/review-yandex-webvisor) — a method for investigating session recordings: define a question, select visits, check observations, and prepare an anonymized report.
- [Video Method Kit](https://github.com/Lisabet/video-method-kit) — skills for planning video stories, scenes, visual consistency, motion, and quality checks.

## Share your experience

If you use one of these tools, I'd love to hear what worked and where you got stuck. Open an issue in the relevant repository with your task, setup, and experience.
